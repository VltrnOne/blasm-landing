## Design Context

### Users
Investors, potential enterprise clients, and strategic partners evaluating BLASM as a consulting engagement or growth-stage investment. They arrive skeptical — they've seen a hundred consulting sites. Context: desktop during business hours, doing due diligence. Mobile secondary.

### Brand Personality
**Kinetic, cross-disciplinary, editorial.** BLASM is a 3-year-old operator-led firm that spans logistics, R&D, and agentic AI — breadth is the moat. Not theoretical consultants; builders who advise. The site should feel like cracking open a Wired issue that's been redesigned by Pentagram.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: Bold operator energy — Bloomberg Terminal meets Wired magazine editorial
- **Reference**: Pentagram / Sagmeister — editorial layouts, unexpected compositions, creative confidence
- **Theme**: Light — warm editorial paper, not AI dark mode
- **Typography**: Oversized condensed headlines that break the grid, refined body text
- **Color**: Warm off-white ground, near-black tinted text, single bold vermillion accent
- **Layout**: Asymmetric, editorial columns, mixed alignment, no card grids
- **Hero**: Scroll-driven video frame sequence as background texture (30 frames from brand video)
- **Differentiator**: Cross-domain range (Logistics + R&D + AI under one roof)

### Design Principles
1. **Editorial, not SaaS** — Treat the page like a magazine spread, not a product landing page
2. **Typography IS the design** — Oversized type, condensed/expanded contrast, type as visual element
3. **Earned asymmetry** — Break the grid with purpose; every layout choice communicates hierarchy
4. **One accent, used sparingly** — Vermillion red-orange, never more than 10% surface area
5. **No AI tells** — No gradient text, no glow cards, no blur orbs, no dark-mode-with-neon
